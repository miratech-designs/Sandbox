5\. Generate thumbnails that actually get clicks (not just “look good”)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Most people completely misunderstand thumbnails. They think it’s about making something that looks nice but that’s not what gets clicks.

A thumbnail has one job: To make someone stop scrolling and feel something instantly.

And most thumbnails fail because they miss the fundamentals:

*   There’s no clear focal point
*   The text is either unreadable or unnecessary
*   The emotion is flat
*   Everything blends into the background

So even if it looks “decent,” it doesn’t convert, and that’s where ChatGPT Images 2.0 becomes insanely useful.

> **_Prompt:_**
> 
> _Create a high-CTR YouTube thumbnail for a video titled: “AI will replace programmers”_
> 
> _Make sure the thumbnail includes:_
> 
> • _Strong emotional expression (fear, surprise, urgency)  
> _• _Large, bold, instantly readable text  
> _• _A single clear focal point with high contrast  
> _• _Clean composition optimized for small screens_

What you’ll notice is that it doesn’t just generate a random image but a thumbnail that you actually wants to use.

6\. Create visual SOPs that people actually follow
--------------------------------------------------

This one looks simple on the surface, but it’s way more powerful than it sounds.

Because most SOPs don’t fail due to bad information, they fail because nobody wants to read them.

I am talking about the long text instructions, messy steps, unclear flow — people skim them, misunderstand them, and then make mistakes.

So instead of writing: “How to do X”, and creating an SOP that no one wants to read try ChatGPT Images 2.0.

> **_Here’s a prompt:_**
> 
> _Create a visual step-by-step guide for: “How to post on LinkedIn daily”_
> 
> _Make sure it includes:_
> 
> • _Clear step-by-step flow  
> _• _Minimal text per step  
> _• _Simple diagrams or icons  
> _• _Logical progression from idea → writing → posting_
