This matters because it fights one of the biggest AI mistakes people make:
They look for exciting ideas instead of painful ones.
But money usually lives in boring work.
Support.
Research.
Summaries.
Reporting.
Scheduling.
Invoicing.
Documentation.
Workflow cleanup.
And that is the clue.
AI becomes valuable when it removes drag.
You can see the same pattern in Anthropic’s Gumroad customer story. Gumroad described using Claude for real product and support work, and Anthropic says the company shipped features four times faster with Claude 3.5 Sonnet in that workflow.
That is the monetization lesson most prompt lists miss.
Do not ask Claude, “What business should I start?”
Ask Claude to find existing, painful work that already costs someone time or money and can be made easier with AI.
That is a much better filter.
